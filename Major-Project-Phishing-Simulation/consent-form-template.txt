STUDY OVERVIEW

This is an educational cybersecurity awareness study designed to measure and 
improve employee resilience to phishing attacks. You may receive a fake phishing 
email as part of this training exercise. The goal is NOT to punish, but to educate.

---

WHAT WILL HAPPEN

Over the next 10 days, you may receive:

1. ONE PHISHING SIMULATION EMAIL that mimics a real phishing attack
   • The email will ask you to "verify your credentials" or "confirm your password"
   • It will contain a link to a fake login page
   • The email will come from a spoofed sender address (e.g., "support@company-internal.test")

2. A FAKE LOGIN PAGE (if you click the link)
   • It will look like a real company login page
   • It will ask for your username and password
   • ⚠️  DO NOT enter your real password — only test credentials if desired

3. DATA COLLECTION (if you interact with the phishing email)
   • Timestamp of when you clicked the link
   • Timestamp of any form submission
   • Your email address (if you submitted the form)
   • No other personal or financial data will be collected

4. AWARENESS TRAINING (if you fell for the simulation)
   • If you clicked the link or submitted credentials, you'll be invited to a 
     remedial training session
   • This training is educational and supportive, NOT punitive
   • All staff receive training, regardless of performance

---

WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR DATA

✓ Your responses will be logged ANONYMOUSLY
  • Individual results will NOT be publicly shared or used against you
  • Results will be AGGREGATED (combined across departments/roles)
  • No individual employee names appear in reports

✓ Your data is CONFIDENTIAL
  • Only the IT Security team and training coordinator will see individual results
  • Data will be deleted [TIMELINE, e.g., "within 30 days of study conclusion"]

✓ No REAL passwords or sensitive data will be collected
  • The fake login page will accept any test username/password
  • Real credential submissions will NOT be processed or stored

  line "Opt Out of Phishing Simulation"

  employment or evaluations

---

WHAT YOU SHOULD DO IF YOU RECEIVE THE PHISHING EMAIL

DO:
  ✓ Read the email carefully — look for red flags (urgent language, fake sender, 
    suspicious links)
  ✓ If you recognize it as phishing, REPORT it to IT: security@company.com
  ✓ If you click the link by accident, just close the page — no harm done
  ✓ If you submit test credentials, don't worry — they won't be used maliciously

DON'T:
  ✗ Do NOT enter your real password on the fake login page
  ✗ Do NOT panic or feel embarrassed if you click it
  ✗ Do NOT forward the phishing email outside the company
